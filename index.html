Help & shortcuts

Tools (left bar)

  • Select & editV
  • Draw wallsW
  • Draw a rectangular roomR
  • Place the personP

Drawing walls

  • Place pointsclick
  • Lock angle to 45° stepsShift
  • Finish the chaindouble-click, Enter or right-click
  • Close a loopclick the start point
  • CancelEsc

Points snap to the grid and to existing wall ends; the cursor ring turns orange on a wall end. Room tool: click two opposite corners, or drag.

Editing

  • Select a wallclick it
  • Move a cornerdrag a blue handle
  • Delete selected wallDel
  • DeselectEsc
  • Undo / redoCtrl+Z Ctrl+Y

Walls joined at a corner move together. Height, thickness and colour are in the Selected wall card.

Camera & views

  • Orbitdrag (Select tool) or middle-drag
  • Panright-drag
  • Zoommouse wheel
  • Home: fit the whole sceneH
  • Top viewT
  • Parallel projectionO
  • Grid on / offG

Eye view

  • Enter / exitE
  • Look arounddrag
  • WalkWASD or arrows
  • RunShift
  • JumpSpace
  • Field of viewmouse wheel

Walls block you. Without a placed person, eye view drops one at the centre of the view.

Files

  • Save designCtrl+S
  • Open designCtrl+O or drop a file

A design file holds the whole scene, including every changed setting: send it to someone and they can open it here. Clear removes all walls and the person (undoable). The camera button saves a clean image of the 3D view.

Panels

Performance

If it feels slow, lower Corner shading (AO) or turn off Realistic rendering in the Rendering card. Click the FPS readout (bottom right) for a diagnostics report.
