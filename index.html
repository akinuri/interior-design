Help & shortcuts

Tools (left bar)

  • SelectV
  • LineL
  • RectangleR
  • CircleC
  • Push / PullP
  • PersonU

Draw a flat shape on the ground or on any face, then push / pull it into 3D. Person tool: drag while placing to set which way they face.

Drawing

  • Line: place pointsclick
  • Close into a faceclick the start point
  • Stop drawing linesdouble-click, Enter or right-click
  • Rectangle: two cornersclick-click or drag
  • Circle: centre, then radiusclick-click or drag
  • Line 45° / square rectangleShift
  • CancelEsc

Draw on the ground or on the face under the cursor (walls too). Lines across a face split it; closed loops become faces. Points snap to corners (orange ring), midpoints (teal), edges (pink) and the grid.

Push / Pull

  • Move a facedrag it, or click, move, click
  • Face of a solidout adds, in carves
  • Push to the far sidecuts a hole (door, window)
  • CancelEsc or right-click

A loose face grows a solid and stays as its base. Draw a shape on a face first to push / pull just that part. The distance snaps to the level of other corners.

Exact values

  • Line lengthtype 2.5, Enter
  • Rectangle sizetype 4;3, Enter
  • Circle radiustype 0.6, Enter
  • Circle sidestype 24 before the first click, or 24s while drawing
  • Push / pull distancetype 2.7, Enter

Type while drawing or pushing; the value shows next to the cursor. Rectangle: width;depth (4,3 also works).

Editing

  • Select a face or edgeclick it (Select tool)
  • … with its edges / facesdouble-click
  • … with everything connectedtriple-click
  • Add / add or removeCtrl / Shift + click
  • Edge loop / face loopAlt + click
  • Edge ringCtrl + Alt + click
  • Select allCtrl+A
  • Delete selectedDel
  • DeselectEsc
  • Undo / redoCtrl+Z Ctrl+Y

Change the colour of the selected faces in the Selection card. Deleting a face keeps its edges; deleting the edge between two flat faces merges them. Back faces show in their own colour (Rendering card); Reverse faces turns them around. Smooth blends the shading across edges where faces meet at less than its angle (a hexagon becomes round); circles with more than 12 sides come out smooth.

Shapes block you. Without a placed person, eye view drops one at the centre of the view.

Files

  • Save designCtrl+S
  • Open designCtrl+O or drop a file

A design file holds the whole scene, including every changed setting: send it to someone and they can open it here. Clear removes all shapes, lines and the person (undoable). The camera button saves a clean image of the 3D view.

Performance

If it feels slow, lower Corner shading (AO) or turn off Realistic rendering in the Rendering card. Click the FPS readout (bottom right) for a diagnostics report.
